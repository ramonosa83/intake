The Ceiling

Where you keep landing

Your nervous system has chosen a level it believes is safe — and quietly holds you there. In money, yes. But also in visibility, in finishing, in how seen you let yourself be. Let's find it.

The level you keep arriving at the edge of and stepping back from. It might be a number. It might be the visible move, the finished thing, the version of you in your journal who never quite shows up. Say it in your words.

The Body

What your system does

This was never a mindset problem. It lives in your body. Let's listen to it.

The tightness in the chest. The "yeah, but." The flutter of dread. The eye-roll. The going-numb. Notice it and describe it.

The Origin

Where it was set

A set-point this strong was usually installed long ago. This is the tender part — take your time.

Whatever surfaces first. It's surfacing for a reason.
Scarcity or plenty? Tension or ease? What did the adults around you believe about money, about being seen, about people who had more?
Maybe someone was punished for shining. Maybe you got more than a sibling and felt guilt. Maybe success cost someone you loved. If a scene comes up, describe it.
This is the question that matters most. Part of you wants it badly — and part of you is bracing against it. What is that part afraid of? Being seen? Envy? Outgrowing people? Becoming someone you don't recognize? Let it speak.
